Support

MarketMind has no account and no sign-in, so most questions are about data rather than access.

Contact

Open a support ticket. We usually reply within two working days. Please include your app version (shown at the bottom of the app's About tab) and the instrument symbol involved.

Common questions

A price looks wrong or is out of date

Every quote records which provider served it. Market data can be delayed, and some sources publish once a day rather than continuously — a Treasury yield updates on the Federal Reserve's schedule, not tick by tick. MarketMind is an information tool; verify anything you intend to act on against an authoritative source.

An instrument shows a price but a flat chart

Some sources publish a current price with no history at all. Those instruments accumulate a chart from the day they are first tracked rather than showing invented data.

Why are gains blue instead of green?

The conventional green/red pair is nearly indistinguishable for people with red-green colour blindness — around 8% of men. Blue and red are clearly separable for everyone, and every number also carries an explicit sign, so direction never depends on colour alone.

Alerts are not arriving

Check that notifications are enabled for MarketMind in iOS Settings. Alerts also have a cooldown, so a market-wide move sends one digest rather than a notification per instrument.

The Home Screen widget is not updating

iOS controls how often widgets may refresh and reduces this when battery is low or the app is used rarely. The widget shows the age of its data in the corner, and falls back to the last values it received rather than showing an error.

How do I delete my data?

Open the About tab and tap Delete my data. Your alert rules, their history and your push token are erased from our server immediately.

Not financial advice

MarketMind does not provide investment advice or recommendations, does not connect to any brokerage or wallet, and cannot see your holdings. You are responsible for your own investment decisions.
