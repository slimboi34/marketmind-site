MarketMind Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

MarketMind shows market data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no advertising.

This policy describes the only data the app handles.

What we collect

A random install ID. The first time the app runs it generates a random

identifier and sends it with its requests, so that alert rules you create belong

to your copy of the app and nobody else can see or delete them. It is not

derived from your device, your Apple ID or anything about you, and it is not

linked to your name, email or any other identifier, because we do not collect

those.

Push notification token. If you allow notifications, Apple issues your

device an APNs token and the app sends it to our server, together with the

install ID, so that alerts from your rules can be delivered to you. Stored with

the token: the platform (iOS), the app version, and the date the device was last

seen. Nothing else.

Alert rules you create. The thresholds and instruments you choose to watch

are stored on our server so alerts can be evaluated while the app is closed.

Your watchlist stays on your device. When the app or the widget fetches

prices, the symbols on it are sent in the request, and are not stored.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • No account credentials — there are no accounts
  • No contacts, photos, location, health, or financial account data
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA), and no third-party analytics or ad SDKs
  • No browsing history, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind

MarketMind does not connect to your brokerage, bank, or wallet, and cannot

see your holdings, balances, or trades.

Tracking

We do not track you. We do not share any data with data brokers or advertising

networks, and we do not use your data to build a profile.

Third-party market data

Prices come from public market data providers (CoinGecko, the Federal Reserve

Bank of St. Louis (FRED), the European Central Bank via Frankfurter, and

others). Our servers request that data — **your device does not contact those

providers directly**, and no information about you is sent to them.

Data retention and deletion

Alert history is deleted automatically after 30 days. Push tokens are retained

while the device stays registered; deleting the app stops delivery, Apple then

reports the token as invalid and we deactivate it.

To delete everything immediately, open the app's About tab and tap

Delete my data. That erases your alert rules, their alert history and your

push token from our server at once.

Children

MarketMind is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect

data from them.

Not financial advice

MarketMind is an information tool. It does not provide investment advice or

recommendations, and nothing in it is an offer to buy or sell any security or

asset. Market data may be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Verify anything you

intend to act on with an authoritative source. You are solely responsible for

your investment decisions.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date, and where

significant, announced in the app.
